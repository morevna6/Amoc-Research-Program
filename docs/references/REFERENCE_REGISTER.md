Current Use Rules

At this stage:

Scientific assessments support calibrated statements about confidence and projected change.

Official observing-programme pages support descriptions of instruments, arrays, coverage, and methodology.

Institutional summaries provide orientation but should not replace primary papers during later analytical work.

Peer-reviewed reviews provide scientific context and identify relevant primary literature.

Quantitative analysis will later cite the exact dataset release, DOI, version, access date, and methodology.

Conflicting sources will be retained and compared rather than removed to produce artificial agreement.

Planned Expansion During Module 0.3

The formal literature-management system will add:

thematic tags;

evidence type;

geographic and temporal scope;

verification status;

Until that system is established, this register functions as a transparent preliminary inventory.
